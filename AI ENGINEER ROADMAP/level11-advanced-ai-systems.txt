Level 11 · Advanced AI Systems (Multimodal)

Scope: work across modalities and at the current shippable frontier.

M11.1 · Multimodal foundations — (sessions: 1)

Explain, intuitively, how multimodal models fuse text with other modalities.

M11.2 · Vision & document understanding — (sessions: 3)

Do image Q&A and document/PDF understanding, including charts and tables.
Build visual RAG.

M11.3 · Speech: STT & TTS — (sessions: 2)

Build speech-to-text (Whisper) and text-to-speech (Kokoro) pipelines.

M11.4 · Realtime voice agents — (sessions: 3)

Use a realtime API with turn-taking, interruption, and low latency.

M11.5 · Image generation — (sessions: 1)

Use generation models and controllable-generation techniques.

M11.6 · Deep research agents — (sessions: 2)

Build long-horizon research and synthesis into structured reports.

M11.7 · On-device / edge — (sessions: 1)

Run small quantized models locally and reason about the tradeoffs.

M11.8 · Capstone: voice agent or multimodal pipeline — (sessions: 1)

Ship one end-to-end multimodal build.
