Level 12 · Fine-Tuning

Scope: the prompt-vs-RAG-vs-fine-tune call, and a small tune when an eval justifies it.

M12.1 · Should you fine-tune? — (sessions: 1)

Make the tradeoff honestly; identify the narrow cases where FT wins.

M12.2 · Data curation for fine-tuning — (sessions: 2)

Build and format high-quality training data (most of the real work).

M12.3 · SFT & LoRA/QLoRA — (sessions: 3)

Run a LoRA/QLoRA tune with PEFT (Unsloth/Axolotl) and set hyperparameters.

M12.4 · Preference tuning: DPO — (sessions: 2)

Build preference data and run DPO; know when it's warranted.

M12.5 · Reinforcement fine-tuning (RFT) — (sessions: 1)

Explain reward-based tuning intuition and its use cases.

M12.6 · Distillation — (sessions: 1)

Distill a large model into a small one for cost/latency wins.

M12.7 · Evaluating fine-tunes — (sessions: 1)

Measure before/after on Level-7 evals and catch regressions.

M12.8 · Capstone: a LoRA that beats a prompted baseline — (sessions: 1)

Ship a tune with the eval numbers, before and after, as proof.
